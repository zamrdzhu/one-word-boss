One word for the boss: Reply-all. Every thought becomes a broadcast, every "quick question" reaches the whole company, and nothing is ever just between two people.

## The word

> Reply-all

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one word and writes the same word (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this word

It is funny because everyone has lived it: the boss who turns a private answer into a company-wide event. One hyphenated word captures the whole management style — loud, indiscriminate, impossible to ignore — without naming a single flaw directly. It rings true on its own, needs no explanation, and every office worker recognises the person instantly, usually while sighing at their inbox.
